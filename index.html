Candidate Biography

Hon. Zaharadeen Idris Mato (Mai Matasa)

State House of Assembly Contestant • Birnin-Kudu Constituency, Jigawa State

Hon. Zaharadeen Idris Mato, popularly known as Mai Matasa, is a banking professional, financial strategist, and grassroots community development advocate from Birnin-Kudu Constituency.

His professional experience within the banking and financial services sector has contributed to his deep understanding of financial management, economic activities, businesses, entrepreneurship, and the critical importance of sustainable community development.

His legislative blueprint and public service focus centers on championing transformative initiatives for Birnin-Kudu through dedicated attention to:
